## 0.1.0

* Initial release.
* Universal Links (iOS, app and UIScene lifecycles), Android App Links and URI schemes.
* Deferred deep linking: Google Play install referrer, iOS pasteboard (opt-in) and fingerprint matching.
* Short links with aliases, offline long-link fallback, events, identity and QR code URLs.
* `TwigConfig.transport` to send requests through your own HTTP client (for example Dio).
